ORDER

1. Petitioner has filed this writ petition seeking a direction
   to the respondents to dispose of his representation dated
   02.01.2026 regarding regularization of his stay status.

2. Despite a reasonable period having elapsed since the said
   representation, no decision has been communicated to the
   petitioner.

3. Learned counsel for the respondents seeks four weeks to
   file a compliance affidavit indicating the steps taken on
   the said representation.

4. Issue notice. The respondents are directed to file a
   compliance affidavit detailing the steps taken on the
   petitioner's grievance within 30 days from today. The
   affidavit shall be sworn by an officer not below the rank
   of Deputy Secretary.

5. List on the next date of hearing.

                                            (M. Kapoor, J.)
